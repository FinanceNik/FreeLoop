## What
A person who reads German, and one who reads Dutch, uses the packaged app in their language and corrects
the catalogues: awkward or wrong terms, inconsistent words for the same thing, anything that addresses the
user, and anything that reads badly once laid out.

## Acceptance
- The packaged app has been used in Deutsch and in Nederlands across the main views, the menu bar and a
  notification.
- Corrections are made in the catalogues and the issue lists what changed.

## Notes
Needs a look at the running app and a native reader's judgement, so it is not for an unattended run.
