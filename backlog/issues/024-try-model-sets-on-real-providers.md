## What
In the rebuilt app, make a set that plans on Opus or Fable and works on a small model such as Qwen 3.8 Flash, give it to a workflow, plan a small feature and run its issues AFK.

## Acceptance
- The small model's provider lists the model in the dialog, or "Other model…" reaches it.
- The planning stages ran on the big model and the issues on the small one, as the transcripts and the Board say.
- The run's costs look right against the provider's own billing.

## Notes
- Needs real keys, so a person does it.
